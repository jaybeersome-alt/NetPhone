# NetPhone Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 18, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

NetPhone is an Android messaging app that lets users exchange messages using randomly assigned in-app identifiers. These identifiers are for use within NetPhone only. They are not real telephone numbers.

This Privacy Policy explains what information NetPhone processes and how it is used.

## 2. Information We Process

When you use NetPhone, the app and its supporting services may process:

- **Account information:** A Firebase Authentication user identifier used to identify your account. The current app supports anonymous sign-in.
- **In-app identifier:** The NetPhone identifier assigned to your account.
- **Messages:** Message text, sender and recipient account identifiers, and message timestamps.
- **Technical information:** Information that Firebase, Supabase, or their infrastructure may process to operate, secure, and troubleshoot their services.

Do not send passwords, financial information, or other sensitive personal information in messages.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:
- Create and identify accounts.
- Assign and retain in-app identifiers.
- Deliver and retrieve messages between users.
- Maintain, troubleshoot, and protect the service.

## 4. Storage and Service Providers

NetPhone uses Google Firebase Authentication and Supabase to support authentication, identifier assignments, and messaging.

Information may be processed or stored on their infrastructure. These providers have their own privacy policies and practices. Review their policies to understand how they handle information.

Messages are not necessarily end-to-end encrypted. Do not assume that messages are private from the service operators or infrastructure providers.

## 5. Sharing Information

NetPhone uses its service providers to operate the app. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to address security issues, abuse, or protection of users and the service.

This policy does not promise that information can never be accessed or disclosed.

## 6. Retention and Deletion

Account identifiers and messages may remain stored until they are deleted or the service's data-retention practices change.

NetPhone currently may not provide an in-app account or message deletion feature. To ask about deleting your information, contact the developer using the contact information provided with the app. A request may require verification and may be subject to legal or technical limitations.

## 7. Children's Privacy

NetPhone is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you are a parent or guardian concerned about information associated with a child, contact the developer.

## 8. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect stored information and restrict unauthorized access. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as NetPhone changes. The latest version should be published at the same public URL. Material changes should be communicated when required by applicable law.
